about

dEUSeXmACHINAfilms makes dance films, experimental and hybrid documentary, and music videos.

Founded in 2018, working between Chile and Austria.

Deus ex machina is a Latin expression for a dramatic and scenographic device of Greek tragedy: a god, lowered onto the stage by a machine, who resolves human conflicts. For us, the camera is a kind of mechanical god, capable of creating unique realities that allow humans to reflect and, eventually, resolve their conflicts.

The work has screened at more than 40 festivals in 18 countries: Chile, Argentina, Peru, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Costa Rica, Mexico, the United States, Canada, Spain, Portugal, Italy, Greece, Ukraine, Romania, Indonesia and Australia.

misoginia Pandora won Best Film at DJK Fest Imagitari, Indonesia. Al aire el río, for the band SUBE, won the Animal and Environmental Film Festival, Mexico. ESCAPE, produced with Heidi Duckler Dance, received an honorable mention at the Mexico City Videodance Festival, and Morar won Best Choreography at the VIII Muestra Movimiento Audiovisual.
