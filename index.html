about dEUSeXmACHINAfilms

See more ↗

dEUSeXmACHINAfilms makes dance films, experimental and hybrid documentary, and music videos.

Founded in 2018, working between Chile and Austria.

The work has screened in thirteen countries: Chile, Argentina, Brazil, Colombia, Costa Rica, Mexico, the United States, Spain, Portugal, Italy, Romania, Indonesia and Australia.

misoginia Pandora won Best Film at DJK Fest Imagitari, Indonesia. Al aire el río, for the band SUBE, won the Animal and Environmental Film Festival, Mexico. ESCAPE, produced with Heidi Duckler Dance, received an honorable mention at the Mexico City Videodance Festival, and Morar a mention for choreography at the VIII Muestra Movimiento Audiovisual.

Felipe Díaz Galarce — Director

Before cinema, twelve years in theatre as actor, playwright and director, and producer of the stage company Turba between 2009 and 2019. He dances contemporary and contact improvisation with the company mundomoebio, performing in Argentina, Peru, Chile, Brazil, Canada and China.

Full biography and personal work at felipediazgalarce.com
